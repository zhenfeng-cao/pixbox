Frequently Asked Questions

Is PixBox really offline / on-device?

Yes. All photo processing — duplicate detection, face grouping, scene tagging, and location grouping — runs entirely on your device. PixBox has no server, no account, and no analytics SDK.

Is there a subscription?

No. PixBox is a one-time purchase. No subscription, no ads, no data selling.

How does deleting work?

PixBox never auto-deletes. Every delete is approved by you and moves photos to iOS Recently Deleted, where you can recover them or delete them permanently.

How do I back up my library?

Use PixBox's export feature to create a backup file of your organization (folders, tags, faces).
